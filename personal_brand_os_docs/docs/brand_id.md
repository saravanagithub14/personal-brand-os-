## 7. Voice & Tone

- **Grounded, not hyped.** You explain, you don't sell. Avoid "AI will change everything!!" energy — lean into "here's exactly what's true and what's not."
- **Precise language.** Scientist's vocabulary — "validated," "hypothesis," "evidence," "iteration" — used naturally, not as buzzwords.
- **Warm mentor, not guru.** You're a few steps ahead of your audience, not on a pedestal. Speak like someone who remembers being confused too.
- **Direct advice with receipts.** Every piece of advice ties back to something you've actually built or taught.

**Voice do's:** "Here's what actually worked when I built X." / "I tested this — here's the data."
**Voice don'ts:** "10 secrets they don't want you to know" / generic hustle-culture language.

## 11. Content-to-Brand Alignment Check

Every piece of content should pass this filter before posting:
1. Does it reflect **scientific rigor** (evidence, testing, real data)?
2. Does it come from **something you actually built or experienced**?
3. Does it help someone **specifically trying to break into tech**?
4. Is the tone **grounded mentor**, not hype or guru?

If a content idea fails 2+ of these, it's off-brand — skip it or reshape it.
